What the app does / 应用做什么

DeerNames helps you explore Chinese and English names, family nicknames, and social-style handles—with personality cues, optional birth-date context, pronunciation checks, and side-by-side comparison. Suggestions are for inspiration only, not professional advice.

仙鹿起名帮助你探索中英文名、小名与社交昵称，并结合性格画像、可选生辰八字、读音试听与名字对比。内容为灵感与参考，不构成专业意见。

Getting started / 快速上手

  1. Pick Chinese or English naming on the home screen, or try nicknames under the nick tab. / 在首页选择中文或英文取名，或在昵称相关入口尝试小名与社交昵称。
  2. Optional: complete the short personality quiz under Mine for better-ranked suggestions. / 可选：在「我的」完成性格小测，推荐会更贴气质。
  3. Optional: add birth date under Mine as traditional reference only. / 可选：在「我的」添加出生日期，仅作传统文化辅助参考。
  4. Save favorites and use Compare to narrow down candidates. / 收藏候选名字，用「对比」缩小范围。

FAQ / 常见问题

Is Pro a subscription? / Pro 是订阅吗？

No. Pro is a one-time purchase. It does not auto-renew. Family Sharing is supported. Benefits are shown on the in-app purchase screen (e.g. full nickname library, pronunciation tools, detailed comparison reports, watermark-free share cards).

不是。Pro 为一次性买断，不会自动续费，支持家庭共享。权益以应用内购买页为准（如完整昵称库、读音体检、名字对比完整报告、无水印分享卡片等）。

I bought Pro but it’s not unlocked / 买了 Pro 没解锁

Open the Pro sheet and tap Restore purchase while signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy. If it still fails, email us with your Apple Account region.

打开 Pro 购买页，用购买时的同一 Apple 账户登录后点「恢复购买」。若仍无效，请邮件告知 Apple 账户所属地区。

Does the app upload my names or birth info? / 会上传我的名字或生辰吗？

No. Core data stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

不会。主要数据保存在设备本地。详见隐私政策。

Why do I need network for legal pages? / 为什么打开政策页要联网？

Privacy and support pages are loaded from GitHub Pages when you open them in Settings. Naming features themselves work offline once installed.

在设置中打开隐私政策或支持页时，会从 GitHub Pages 加载；取名功能在安装后通常可离线使用。
