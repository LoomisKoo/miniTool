DeerNames Privacy Policy

仙鹿起名 · Effective date: 2026-09-22

Contact / 联系我们

Email: magaxy@163.com

Summary / 概要

DeerNames is designed to respect your privacy. The app runs primarily on your device.

仙鹿起名尊重你的隐私。本应用主要在设备本地运行。

Information we handle / 我们处理什么信息

  • Names, personality answers, optional birth details, saved favorites, and preferences are stored locally on your device by default. We do not upload them to our servers.
  • No account sign-up. We do not actively collect your name, email, contacts, precise location, or photo library contents.
  • Photo access is used only when you choose to save a generated share card to your photo library.

你输入的姓名、性格答案、可选的生辰信息、收藏与应用偏好默认保存在设备本地，不会上传到我们的服务器。不要求注册账号，也不主动收集邮箱、通讯录、精确位置或照片内容。照片权限仅用于将你主动保存的分享卡片写入相册。

Purchases / 购买

Pro purchases are processed by Apple App Store. We do not receive your payment details. The app only reads Apple’s purchase status to unlock or restore Pro features.

Pro 购买由 Apple App Store 处理，我们不接触支付信息。应用只读取 Apple 提供的购买状态，用于解锁和恢复 Pro。

Third-party services / 第三方服务

The app may use Apple StoreKit for in-app purchases and system speech services for pronunciation playback. Those services are subject to Apple’s terms and privacy policy.

Opening this policy or the support page in the app requires an internet connection; the pages are hosted on GitHub Pages.

应用通过 StoreKit 处理内购，并通过系统语音提供读音试听，受 Apple 条款约束。在应用内打开本页或支持页需要网络连接；页面托管在 GitHub Pages。

Deletion / 删除数据

Deleting the app removes its local data. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple purchase; use Restore purchase in the app if needed.

删除应用会清除本地数据，不会自动取消 Apple 购买；可在应用内使用「恢复购买」。

Children & sensitive info / 儿童与敏感信息

Please do not enter unnecessary sensitive personal information. Optional birth details are used only for on-device naming suggestions.

请勿输入不必要的敏感个人信息。生辰信息为可选的取名辅助，仅用于设备上的推荐计算。

Changes / 政策变更

We may update this policy as the product or law changes. The effective date at the top will be revised when we do.

我们可能根据功能或法律变化更新本政策，届时会更新文首生效日期。
